# Kaleva Tasku (Tasku-Uutiset) 🗞️📱
> **JunctionX Oulu 2026 • Challenge 2: NEWS FOR EVERYONE**  
> *A community-driven, agentic multilingual newsroom bridging 125 years of Kaleva's trusted journalism with zero-translation visual situational awareness.*

---

## ❄️ The Challenge & Problem
In Northern Finland, 5,000+ international university students, Nokia ICT engineers, and seasonal workers live in an information vacuum during severe sub-zero weather and civic emergencies.
- **Raw machine translation fails on civic reality:** Terms like *Pääkallokeli*, *Baana*, *PSOAS*, or *Kela* carry life-critical legal and safety context that literal translation distorts.
- **Sub-zero touchscreen friction:** At −25°C at an Oulu bus stop, bare fingers freeze within 40 seconds.
- **Paywall disconnection:** Traditional consumer subscriptions (€15–20/mo) result in 0% expat penetration, forcing newcomers into unverified WhatsApp/Jodel rumor channels.

---

## ⚡ The Solution: Dual-Engine Architecture

### Engine 1: Mobile Tasku Micro-Cards (Situational Ergonomics)
- **Orange Verified Masthead:** `KALEVA TASKU • La 24.10.2026` — editorial authenticity and institutional trust.
- **Monochromatic Line-Art Situation Diagrams:** Clean vector graphics decode complex civic incidents in < 2 seconds:
  $$\text{Problem / Häiriö} \longrightarrow \mathbf{\color{#22c55e}\text{Salad-Green Arrow}} \longrightarrow \text{Actionable Solution / Ratkaisu}$$
- **Korva Audio Stream (`(30s)`):** One-tap 30-second spoken summary directly to earbuds so winter gloves stay on.
- **Direct Civic Action Relays:** Real-time routing for Fintraffic Kaakkuri bypass, Police 112 status, and OSL Baana winter cycling plowing.

### Engine 2: Classic Broadsheet 1899 View
- Clicking any headline-link seamlessly unfolds the story into an authentic multi-column newspaper article.
- Preserves full journalistic depth, reporter bylines, and Council for Mass Media (**JSN § 10**) ethics compliance.

## 🤖 3-Agent Autonomous Editorial Pipeline + Cockpit
```
[Kaleva Wire / Police / OSL / Fintraffic]
                  │
                  ▼
         [Agent 1: Ingest & Triage]
  (Extracts severity, geolocation, civic impact)
                  │
                  ▼
      [Agent 2: Ergonomic Synthesizer]
  (Drafts micro-cards, line-art diagrams & 30s audio briefs)
                  │
                  ▼
       [Agent 3: JSN Ethics Verifier]
  (Checks against Finnish Council for Mass Media rules)
                  │
                  ▼
   [Editor Cockpit (Human-in-the-Loop)]
 (Desk editors review diffs and approve with 1-click)
                  │
                  ▼
         [Published to Tasku]
```

## 💼 Business Model: B2B Institutional Civic SaaS
Instead of charging price-sensitive students, Kaleva Tasku licenses bulk civic access to the institutions bearing direct duty-of-care liability:
- **University of Oulu & OAMK:** €35,000 / yr (International student retention, campus safety)
- **Nokia & Oulu ICT Corporates:** €25,000 / yr (Turnkey expat civic onboarding)
- **City of Oulu & Oulu 2026 Foundation:** €40,000 / yr (European Capital of Culture civic guide for 300,000 visitors)
- **Lapland Tourism Consortia:** €14,000 / yr (Seasonal worker logistics & safety)
- **Total Year 1 ARR:** **€114,000**
- **Unit Economics:** €18,240 annual COGS yields **84.0% Gross Margin** and Day 1 breakeven.
- **Zero Cannibalization:** Acts as top-of-funnel acquisition converting expats into €19/mo lifelong Kaleva subscribers.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack
- **Frontend:** React 18, TypeScript, TailwindCSS, Lucide Icons, Vite
- **Arctic Voice Engine:** Web Speech API & Edge Speech Synthesis (Finnish audio briefs)
- **Pipeline Relays:** Simulated real-time feeds from `api.ouka.fi` (OSL transit) and Fintraffic
- **Typography:** Authentic Kaleva styling (Cinzel, Playfair Display, Plus Jakarta Sans)
